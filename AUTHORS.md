# Authors and provenance

## Independent continuation

**Juan Andrés Vallejo Rozo** — maintainer of Cycling Safety ML. This continuation introduces a new modular implementation, raw-sensor quality checks and windowing policy, recording-disjoint evaluation, baseline comparisons, retraining, reproducible inference, tests and documentation.

The work begins from the collaborative CyclingSafety project. It does not claim exclusive authorship of the original idea, recordings, annotations, trained historical models or maps.
